Kasumi 1.0.5: Steam Link finds your PC on more networks.

- Steam Link: if Kasumi can't find your PC, press X and type its address (run `ipconfig` on the PC, it looks like 192.168.1.20). Some routers, guest Wi-Fi and PC firewalls block the search; asking the PC directly gets through. Already paired and the PC's address changed? Press Y in the Steam library, then X
- Steam Link: the search now waits 5 seconds instead of 2, for PCs that are slow to answer
- Clearer messages when the 3DS isn't connected to Wi-Fi, or when nothing answers at the address you typed
- Reports now say why a Steam search came back empty, so problems can be found faster

Update from Settings > Updates, or install Kasumi.cia with FBI. Homebrew Launcher users can replace Kasumi.3dsx. Your login, library and settings are kept.
